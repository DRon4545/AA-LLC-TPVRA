# AA LLC — Vendor Assessment

## 1. Assessment Method

Each question is scored from 1 to 5.

| Score | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Strong control / low risk |
| 2 | Minor weakness |
| 3 | Moderate weakness / partially implemented |
| 4 | Significant weakness |
| 5 | Major deficiency / unacceptable exposure |

Higher scores indicate higher risk.

### Weighted Calculation

`Domain Score = Average Question Score × Domain Weight`

`Overall Vendor Score = Sum of Weighted Domain Scores`
