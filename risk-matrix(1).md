## Inherent vs. Residual Risk

This sample primarily demonstrates **control/residual risk scoring**.

A production TPVRA should separately document:

### Inherent Risk

Risk before considering vendor controls.

Suggested factors:

- Type of service
- Sensitivity of information
- System access
- Privileged access
- Government data exposure
- Business criticality
- Geographic exposure
- Subcontractor dependence
- Regulatory/contractual requirements

### Residual Risk

Risk remaining after considering controls and mitigating measures.

A vendor can have high inherent risk but acceptable residual risk if strong controls are demonstrated.

## Decision Rules

### Low
Normal procurement approval and periodic monitoring.

### Moderate
Business owner approval plus documented remediation.

### High
Security/procurement leadership review, formal remediation plan, and closer monitoring.

### Critical
Do not grant sensitive access until material deficiencies are addressed, unless an authorized decision-maker documents and accepts the risk.
